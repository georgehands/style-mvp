Terms and privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

What you're buying

The Style Blueprint is a personal style plan worked out from your answers: your frame plan, your colours, outfit recipes for each look, and what to buy first. It names garments, colours and fits. It doesn't include specific products, prices or links.

It's a one-off payment of £15. There's no subscription and nothing renews. Your Blueprint opens in the app on the device you paid on as soon as payment goes through.

It's style advice, not a guarantee. What suits you is partly taste, and colour readings from a photo depend on the light.

Refunds

If you're not happy, ask for a refund within 14 days of paying and you'll get your money back in full, no questions asked. Reply to your payment receipt to ask.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

What we collect

  • Your answers (height, shape, measurements, what you'd like to hide or show off, your week and style) are saved in your browser on your device.
  • When you save your profile, your name, email and a summary of your answers are sent to us so we can reach you about your plan. Our website host, Netlify, stores them.
  • Anonymous counts of how many visits reach each step, and which link people came from. These contain no names, emails or answers.
  • Marketing emails only if you tick the box. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your photos

If you scan your face for your colours, the photo is read on your phone and is never uploaded or saved. The face-reading model is downloaded to your phone from Google's servers to do this. Your photo is not sent to them or to us.

If you upload a reference outfit photo, it stays on your device as a small preview in your saved profile. It isn't sent to us.

Deleting your data

"Delete my profile" in the You tab removes everything saved on your device. To delete what we hold (your name, email and answer summary), reply to any email from us or to your payment receipt, and we'll remove it.
